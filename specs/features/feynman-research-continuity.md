# FEYNMAN-RESEARCH-CONTINUITY

## Objective

Complete the Feynman research-continuity tranche inside the existing Nexus research and VAULT architecture:

- Search prior Feynman sessions and resume relevant work.
- Preserve a chronological lab notebook for every run.
- Externalize the research plan and intermediate artifacts with collision-safe session IDs.
- Preserve a separate provenance sidecar.
- Provide protected local HTML preview and PDF export.

## Runtime Contract

- Every `feynman_research` run starts one bounded local session under `agent-workspace/feynman/sessions/`.
- Session IDs are generated by Nexus and accepted only through a strict safe-ID validator.
- The research engine emits best-effort chronological progress events for the workflow and each agent stage.
- A completed session contains a plan, notebook, final report, evidence ledger, claim audit, reviewer findings, provenance sidecar, HTML preview, PDF export, and manifest.
- `feynman_outputs` supports list, search, resume, and export actions while retaining its existing VAULT-backed output behavior.
- `/api/feynman/artifacts` serves only known artifacts from validated session IDs and remains protected and local-only.

## Guardrails

- No arbitrary output directory, filename, or filesystem path may be supplied by a caller.
- No raw local path is exposed through the browser artifact route.
- No cloud, paid service, new dependency, direct provider call, silent execution, or visual redesign.
- Continuity writes are best-effort and must never prevent the research result from returning.
- PDF export is generated locally from the final report.
- Existing Feynman execution and approval gates remain authoritative.
- This tranche does not claim full Feynman source parity while other useful capabilities remain pending.

## Verification
