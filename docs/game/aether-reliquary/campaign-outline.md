# Aether Reliquary Campaign Outline

## Campaign Spine

The campaign is heroic adventure: the hero restores the reliquary network by helping cities choose difficult hope over the Hollow Regent's painless ending.

## Acts

- Prologue - The Day The Bellroot Rang: the player-created hero descends into The Bellroot Vestibule below Veyrhold as a witness, not a soldier. The opening starts with the Descent Ledger identity moment, oath-lamp exploration, Ilo's oracle wake-up, Gate Monolith discovery, Loom-Shard Charm claim, first forge lesson, and only then the first safe combat trial.
- Act I - Relic Awakening: Veyrhold and Thalos Gate establish oaths, civic responsibility, and route unlocks.
- Act II - City Pilgrimage: Mirrorglass Haven, Cinderfall, Orrery Spire, and Verdant Ossuary reveal how each city survived by bending its reliquary.
- Act III - Faction War: Sable Meridian, Ironbloom Bastion, and Nacre Deep threaten to turn the reliquary crisis into a war.
- Act IV - Citadel Breach: Gloamreach and Auric Reliquary convert companion loyalty and recovered truths into the final breach plan.
- Act V - World-Scar Restoration: Ebonwake Citadel tests every city choice and ends with The Hollow Regent.
- Postgame - Relic Trials: rematches, elite affixes, codex completion, reputation completion, cosmetics, and local-first build goals.

## Story Flags

Story should be durable through simple save flags:

- `lore:descent-ledger`, `lore:oath-lamp-arcade`, `npc:oracle-met`, and `lore:gate-monolith` for the non-combat prologue opening.
- `city:*` for city outcomes.
- `route:*` for travel unlocks.
- `companion:*` for recruitment and loyalty.
- `faction:*` for reputation turning points.
- `archive:*` for recovered history.
- `final:*` for finale states.
- `postgame:*` for endgame unlocks.

## Companion Loyalty

Companion loyalty arcs should not be decorative. Each companion needs:

- Recruitment moment.
- Travel banter class/race/city reactions.
- One personal conflict.
- One gameplay perk.
- One loyalty quest.
- One endgame or final-act payoff.

## Final Boss

The Hollow Regent is not a generic evil overlord. The Regent offers peace without pain, choice without risk, and cities without grief. The heroic answer is restoration: the player wins because companions, cities, and recovered truths make hope practical.
