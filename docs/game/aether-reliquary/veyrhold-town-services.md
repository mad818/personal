# MW6FR2 Veyrhold Town Services

## Goal

Veyrhold should be the first release-grade town, not just the first unlocked city card. This slice adds the first playable hub layer: services, district hooks, starter armor/accessory progression, and compact `/hq` proof.

## First Town Services

- Bellroot Anvil: blacksmithing, first tempering, helm/armor fitting, and practical early upgrade language.
- Oath-Lamp Still: alchemy, health/focus vials, and the first condition-cleanse direction.
- Oathmarket Stalls: city scrip, practical rings, amulets, and vendor-discount hooks.
- Pilgrim Rest: checkpoint recovery, rumor gathering, and road preparation.
- Warden Oath Board: civic contracts, local reputation, and first district quest work.

## Starter Gear Progression

The first release now explicitly covers armor and accessory identity:

- Helm: starts as a bland pilgrim helm, then gains oath-bronze visor detail.
- Armor: starts as patched wardplate, then becomes repaired civic plate.
- Gloves: starts as work leather, then gains forge-grip stitching and brass plates.
- Boots: starts as road boots, then gains route-rune straps.
- Rings: starts as plain copper/tin bands, then diverges into market oath and pilgrim-route identities.
- Amulet: starts as cord-and-brass, then grows into oath-lamp pendant language.

Common gear should read practical and understated. Higher-tier items should become visibly distinct through silhouette, material, glow, set marks, and acquisition source.

## Runtime Proof

- `/hq` Map drawer shows Veyrhold services once the town opens.
- `/hq` Map drawer shows starter gear paths for armor and accessories.
- `/hq` Armory drawer shows the same Veyrhold gear plan beside existing Hero Kit and arsenal surfaces.
- Service buttons route through existing local-first actions and drawers: blacksmith to Armory, alchemy/market to Inventory, inn to checkpoint recovery, and oath board to Journal.

## Guardrails

- No `/game` route.
- No cloud save, account system, or multiplayer.
- No fake art replacement.
- No claim that final Veyrhold bespoke maps, audio, vendors, and NPC portraits are finished yet.
- Future 11 cities remain locked previews until their own city slices exist.
