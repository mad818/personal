## Product contract

1. Register all 62 current upstream skill IDs exactly once.
2. Expose every feasible workflow as an original Nexus operating contract with:
   - source category and operating family;
   - unique purpose;
   - required inputs and prerequisites;
   - ordered workflow;
   - guardrails and acceptance checks;
   - truthful native, source-required, connector-required, host-required, or dependency-review availability;
   - exact upstream evidence URL.
3. Expose the active procedures in the existing Skill Library through responsive search, category/family/availability filters, and complete selected detail.
4. Add bounded read-only `list_go_to_market_skills` and `resolve_go_to_market_skill` tools so ORBIT/JANSKY can load the same contract before relevant work.
5. Keep external research, AI generation, browser work, file writes, account actions, posting, PRs, paid calls, and deployment behind their existing protected tools and explicit operator authority.
6. Exclude capabilities whose defining behavior conflicts with Nexus boundaries:
   - autonomous email guessing/enrichment;
   - authenticated-session login bypass scraping;
   - personal-contact lead harvesting;
   - generation of a competing `CLAUDE.md` authority file.
7. Do not install the upstream CLI, marketplace, plugin, skills.sh bundle, dependencies, or host-global files.

## Safety and truth boundaries

- Procedures are read-only guidance, not authority to execute.
- Public-source research must preserve citations, platform terms, rate limits, and unavailable evidence.
- Drafting never implies sending or posting.
- Advertising, outreach, PR, Slack, Notion, Ghost, cloud-storage, and social actions require an available connector plus explicit approval.
- No contact guessing, private-data enrichment, credential reuse, authentication bypass, spam automation, or covert monitoring.
- No copied upstream prompt bodies, code, assets, templates, brand identity, or generated examples.
- No phone/PWA or game/RPG work.

- Marketing and launch work starts from a complete procedure instead of a repository name or a mutable plugin install.
