# Academic Research Source Closure

## Outcome

Close the feasible research and academic-workflow patterns reviewed from
Feynman and Academic Research Skills without turning Nexus into an autonomous
experiment runner or paper-submission system.

## Product behavior

1. Research uses the existing four-stage Researcher, Writer, Verifier, and
   Reviewer pipeline.
2. Direct-source ledgers, claim audits, degraded-stage receipts, workflow output
   contracts, integrity passports, local artifacts, and operator approval gates
   remain the authoritative research behavior.
3. Replication and autoresearch workflows produce bounded, measurable plans
   with stop conditions and approval gates; they do not execute code.
4. Nexus does not silently launch Docker, install dependencies, train models,
   consume paid compute, or mutate experiments.
5. Nexus does not claim PRISMA compliance, learn a personal writing
   fingerprint, generate scientific figures, calibrate reviewer error rates
   without a gold set, or submit manuscripts.
6. Nexus does not silently escalate model tiers or invoke a second model;
   independent-verification posture remains explicit and honest.
7. CC-BY-NC source expression is not copied into the MIT project.

## Verification

- `npm run academic-research:source:check`
- `npm run feynman:check`
- `npm run source:parity:check`
- `npm run type-check`
- focused zero-warning lint

## Benefits

- The useful evidence and integrity workflow stays fully reachable.
- Research cannot quietly become arbitrary code execution.
- Provider choice and Azure credit use remain under operator control.
- Publication-quality claims are not made without the required human,
  full-text, venue, and evaluation evidence.
