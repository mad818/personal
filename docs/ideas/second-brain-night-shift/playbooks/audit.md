# Audit Playbook

## Job

Report second-brain integrity without repairing it.

## Checks

- atoms without source references
- source references that no longer resolve
- atoms without links
- tentative atoms older than 14 days
- unresolved `[FRICTION]` older than 7 days
- threads not updated for 30 days
- pending desk proposals awaiting a human decision

Write a timestamped audit briefing. Do not alter the flagged files.
